Privacy Policy

CleanText Shelf is designed to process text locally in your browser. The text you paste into the tool is not intentionally uploaded to a server by this site.

Text You Paste

The cleaner, preview, redaction, and link-cleaning features run in your browser. We do not store pasted text, cleaned text, or preview output on our server.

Like most websites, the hosting provider may process basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, requested page, timestamp, and error logs to deliver the site and protect the service.

Analytics and Advertising

The current version does not include analytics scripts, advertising scripts, user accounts, payment forms, or newsletter signup forms. If those are added later, this policy should be updated before publishing the change.

Sensitive Information

Although the tool is browser-only, you should still be cautious with highly sensitive personal, legal, medical, financial, or confidential business text. Always review cleaned output before sharing it.
