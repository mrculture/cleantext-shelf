What It Cleans

          

            Zero-width spaces, byte order marks, directional marks, soft hyphens,
            non-breaking spaces, repeated blank lines, smart punctuation, and common
            tracking parameters such as UTM, fbclid, and gclid.
          

        

        

          
Why It Helps

          

            Hidden formatting can break forms, code snippets, CMS editors, CSV files,
            newsletter layouts, and social posts. Cleaning it early saves dull manual fixes.
          

        

        

          
Privacy First

          

            The first version runs entirely in your browser. No accounts, no uploads,
            and no pasted text stored by the site.
